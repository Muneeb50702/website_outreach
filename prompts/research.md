# Research one worthwhile local-business prospect

Start from current public sources and business-owned websites. Prefer an observable website need and a public business contact. Do not scrape Hotfrog or any source prohibiting automated extraction. Record the actual website; no website URL in a listing does not prove the business has no site.

Read outreach-ledger/suppression/policy and avoid known duplicates. Find one business the user can help, verify its services, city/area, public phone/email and availability. Record source URLs, supported facts, checked_at and uncertainty in leads/SLUG.json. A current visual comparison is required before declaring it sales-qualified. Do not invent a recipient, opportunity, ratings or revenue data. Do not send mail or claim a website is built.

Use a safe first-level slug, avoiding apex, www and reserved service names. Summarize the specific improvement and select a distinct design direction appropriate to the business. Hand the validated brief to the next Codex coding session.
